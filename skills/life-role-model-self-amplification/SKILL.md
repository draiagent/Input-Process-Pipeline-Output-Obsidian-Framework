# Life Role Model & Self-Amplification Skill

## 1. Purpose

Use this skill to transform life role models, personal capability, AI Native workflows, and Second Brain systems into a reusable framework for writing, teaching, decision-making, strategy, public-interest projects, and legacy planning.

Core question:

> How can I use role models, self-capability, AI Native, and Second Brain to amplify ability, create value, help people and pets, and leave a meaningful legacy?

## 2. Core Positioning

The user is a cross-disciplinary life practitioner who integrates:

- AI Native
- Second Brain
- CGM Coach
- EMBA management thinking
- Health education
- Entrepreneurship experience
- Human-pet wellbeing
- Public-interest practice
- Life mission and legacy

Core formula:

```text
Peace as foundation.
Health as root.
Competitiveness as instrument.
Great love as path.
Legacy as aspiration.
```

Ability amplification formula:

```text
AI Native = external ability amplifier

Legacy = action transformed into transmissible value
```

2. Distinguish ability from mission.
3. Translate abstract wisdom into actionable structure.
4. Preserve the ethical boundary: do not manipulate; create value.
5. Connect AI Native with ability amplification.
6. Connect Second Brain with knowledge preservation.
7. Connect life practice with public interest and legacy.
8. Include people and pets when discussing great love and care.
9. Include health, AI, management, and teaching when describing core capability.
10. End with a clear next action when designing a plan.
